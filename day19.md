✨ What I Learnt : 
Public accountability sharpens focus  
By posting daily, you’re not just sharing progress—you’re committing to consistency. This builds discipline and keeps your challenge on track.

Documentation = reflection  
Writing about what you did forces you to articulate why you did it. That reflection is where deeper insights emerge (e.g., why a certain prompt worked better, or how Claude’s role-based setup improved outcomes).

Iteration beats perfection  
Each post shows that you don’t need a “finished product” every day. Instead, you’re stacking small improvements—prompt tweaks, project scaffolding, or GitHub commits—that compound over time.

Community engagement adds perspective  
Sharing on LinkedIn invites feedback and visibility. Even if comments are few, the act of publishing helps you see your work from an external lens, which often reveals blind spots.
