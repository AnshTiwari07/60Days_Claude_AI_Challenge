what i learnt : 🎭 Shift in mindset: Instead of just asking AI generic questions, you learned that assigning AI a role (like “Senior Product Manager” or “HR Manager”) dramatically improves the quality of responses.

📈 Benefits you noted: Role-based prompting yields more relevant insights, better recommendations, expert-level thinking, and faster outcomes.

💡 Core principle: AI doesn’t just answer questions—it can perform roles. The better the role you assign, the better the answer you receive.

So, what i really learnt is that prompt engineering through role assignment is a powerful way to unlock expert-level responses from AI, making it more of a collaborator than just a tool.
