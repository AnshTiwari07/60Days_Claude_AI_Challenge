✨ What I Learnt : 
  Consistency compounds results → showing up daily mattered more than intensity.
  
  Context beats length in prompts → I discovered that adding meaningful context made AI outputs sharper and more actionable.
  
  Role assignment works wonders → treating AI as a collaborator (teacher, analyst, coder) unlocked expert‑level insights.
  
  Documentation builds accountability → writing posts and maintaining a repo kept me disciplined and clear.
  
  Iteration > perfection → small daily refinements led to stronger outcomes than chasing flawless results upfront.
  
  Community feedback accelerates growth → sharing publicly brought perspectives I wouldn’t have reached alone.
  
🔑 Reflection line you can add at the end:

  “This challenge wasn’t just about AI — it was about discipline, clarity, and building habits that scale.”
