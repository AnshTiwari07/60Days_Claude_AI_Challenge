What I learned from this post :

AI isn’t just about the tool — the prompt shapes the outcome.

Prompt Engineering is the skill of giving AI clear, specific, and structured instructions.

A vague prompt like “Write a LinkedIn post about AI” produces generic results.

A precise prompt like “Act as a LinkedIn strategist. Write a post about AI productivity for business professionals. Include examples, a call-to-action, and hashtags” delivers targeted, high‑value content.

Why Prompt Engineering matters:
🎯 Better results
⚡ Saves time
📈 Higher productivity

This skill applies across all AI platforms — Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot.

Core principle: The better your prompt, the smarter and more useful the AI response becomes.
