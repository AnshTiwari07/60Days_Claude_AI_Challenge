My 60 days challenge

what i did : https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:share:7470521308187922432/

what i learnt :
  Daily discipline compounds → showing up consistently mattered more than intensity.
  
  Prompt engineering is contextual → I discovered that adding meaningful context beats long prompts every time.
  
  Role assignment works → treating AI as a collaborator (not just a tool) gave me sharper, more relevant outputs.
  
  Public sharing accelerates growth → posting updates on LinkedIn brought feedback and accountability.
  
  Iterative refinement wins → small tweaks each day built stronger results than chasing perfection upfront.

🔑 Reflection
  “This challenge wasn’t just about AI — it was about clarity, discipline, and building habits that scale.”
