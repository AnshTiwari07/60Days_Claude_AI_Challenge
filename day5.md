🌟 Key Learning
Context transforms AI outputs: You discovered that simply asking AI for a roadmap (Prompt A) gives generic results, but adding context (Prompt B) — like your current skills, goals, time availability, and learning style — produces tailored, actionable, and realistic plans.

Prompt ≠ Context: A prompt alone is like giving a destination without directions. Context acts like GPS navigation, guiding AI to deliver outputs that are more personalized and valuable.

Prompt engineering is about quality, not length: You realized that effective prompts aren’t about being longer, but about being richer in meaningful context.

💡 Broader Takeaway
AI is most powerful when you frame the problem with clarity. By embedding context, you help the model understand your unique situation, which makes its responses more relevant whether for learning, career planning, or content creation.

This insight strengthens your skill in prompt engineering — a critical ability for anyone working with AI tools today.
