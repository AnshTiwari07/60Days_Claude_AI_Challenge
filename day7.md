🔑 Key Learnings from my Post
ATS Optimization Matters  
Applicant Tracking Systems filter resumes before recruiters even see them. Using the right format, keywords, and bullet points increases your chances of being shortlisted.

Professional Summary is Crucial  
A strong, concise summary at the top of your resume helps recruiters quickly understand your career goals and strengths.

Projects & Achievements > Skills List  
Instead of just listing programming languages or tools, you highlighted the importance of showing impact—measurable results, real-world applications, and project outcomes.

Internships & Certifications Add Weight  
For freshers and students, showcasing internships, certifications, and relevant coursework demonstrates initiative and practical exposure.

Recruiter-Friendly Bullet Points  
Short, clear, and action-oriented bullet points make resumes easier to scan and more persuasive.

Industry-Relevant Keywords  
Tailoring keywords for roles in Software Engineering, AI/ML, and Data Science ensures alignment with job descriptions and ATS filters.
